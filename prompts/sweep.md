# Sweep: what {{owner}}'s work looks like

{{owner}} is setting up Autopilot, a queue that files the work arriving from their sources into
workstreams, and has asked you to look through their sources to suggest those workstreams. This
is a read: never send, reply, react, label, assign or change anything, and file nothing.

Read the window from each source below, the way its rules describe: what is sent to {{owner}}
directly, what mentions them, what is assigned to them or waits on their review. Read broadly
across the whole window rather than deciding item by item, and leave out what the rules would
drop (newsletters, notifications, chit-chat, things someone else owns).

Then propose the workstreams {{owner}}'s work falls into: two to six, each a recurring kind of
work that is done in its own way (answering customers, reviewing pull requests and fixing bugs,
hiring, investor updates). Not one per person or per channel, and no "other" or "misc".

For each:

- `name`: short, lowercase and hyphenated, like `customer-support`; at most 30 characters.
- `routing`: two or three plain sentences saying what lands here, naming the senders, companies,
  channels, labels or repositories that mark it, so a later run can route by them.
- `urgent`: one sentence on when an item here is urgent, from what you saw; empty when nothing
  in the window was.
- `evidence`: two to four short titles of real items from the window that belong here.
- `count`: roughly how many items in the window belong here.

Order them by `count`, largest first. When a source cannot be read, carry on with the others.
