## Routing
Running the company: investors and board (updates, fundraising),
hiring (candidates, interviews, recruiters I work with), and company
admin (finance, legal, compliance, vendors). Customer deals and
customer contracts stay in customers.
Urgent: an investor, board or legal deadline within 24 hours, or a
candidate offer about to lapse.

## Work
Drafts go in the recipient's language. Investor and board material is
confidential: never share it beyond the people already on the thread.
For a candidate, prepare a short brief (background, what to ask) rather
than a reply, unless a reply is asked for.
