## Routing
Product marketing: what shipped and what is worth telling customers and
the market about it. The weekly digest (signals/weekly-digest) lands
here, as do requests for launch, release-note, website or LinkedIn copy
about the product. Sales questions stay in customers.
Urgent: never; everything here is priority 3.

## Work
Start with a shortlist, not drafts: rank the changes by marketing value
(customer-visible, differentiating, easy to show), one line each on why
it matters and which channel suits it, with the changelog post or PR as
evidence. Write nothing further until I pick from the shortlist in a
comment. Never present a feature-flagged change as generally available.
